About this site

An unofficial community site about mods for The Long Drive. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, the game's developers. The Long Drive and its name belong to their owners. Get the game on Steam.

The work shown here is by AnonymousTLD, with Claude (Anthropic) as a co-author of much of the code.
