TLD RevaMP vs. the base game

TLD RevaMP is a mod for The Long Drive's beta branch by Normal99 and AnonymousTLD: a from-scratch multiplayer and 51 fixes to the game. Everything below is measured in the game.

How it was measured

  • Game: The Long Drive beta (2024.11.26b_test), Unity 2022.3.12f1, BepInEx 5.4.23.5.
  • PC: AMD Ryzen 9 7950X3D, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070, Windows 10. 1280×720, the game's Ultra preset, vsync off, no frame cap.
  • A separate measuring mod drives the runs and records every frame; it works the same with or without RevaMP. The travel runs follow the main road from the start, and the route was checked to be identical in every run.
  • Frame times are measured on the game's main loop (the processor side, where these fixes work).

Multiplayer, rebuilt from scratch

RevaMP doesn't extend the beta's own multiplayer; it replaces it.

  • Host and join from the game's own Multiplayer menu (F11 in game). Steam friends see your game; no port forwarding.
  • Play anywhere on the map: each machine simulates what's near its own player.
  • Shared: players (on foot, seated, driving, as passengers), cars (driving, crashes, damage, parts coming off, fuel and fluids), loose items, building doors and gates, creatures, guns, bullet holes and explosions, sandstorms, tumbleweeds and the radio. Players can stand in a moving bus or on a car.
  • Text chat and proximity voice (heard within 60 m).
  • Mod transfer: a player missing a mod the host uses gets it from the host, if it's on RevaMP's approved list.

Bugs in the base game, fixed

  • Cars could vanish from the road when one of their parts was far away.
  • An emptied bottle came back full (30 L) after reloading.
  • Items fell through the world when you arrived faster than it generated.
  • A bus's rear wheels were saved hanging in the air.
  • Lamps in buildings were lost when the building streamed out first.
  • A car's radiator couldn't explode properly; a worn hat could flood the game with errors.
  • Trailers couldn't be hitched at all in the beta.

Brought back and added

  • Rabbits and sandstorms: still in the beta's files, but nothing started them.
  • Trailers that hitch, brake with the car, and don't creep away when parked.
  • Driving assists (all optional): trailer sway reduction, roll correction, traction control.
  • In-game mod browser (Settings → Revamp → Mods): install, update and remove approved mods (next release).
  • One-key bug reports (Page Down): a zip with a screenshot, logs and your hardware.

Get it

  1. Switch The Long Drive to the beta branch: Steam → right-click the game → Properties → Betas → beta.
  2. Download the latest RevaMP release and follow the INSTALL.txt inside.
  3. Back up Documents\TheLongDrive\Saves first: these are test builds.
